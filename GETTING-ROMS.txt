Cybiko ROM images are not included in this project.

You need to provide your own Classic (V1) dumps:

  boot.bin   Cybiko boot ROM, 32 KB (often named cyrom112.bin)
  flash.bin  CyOS 1.2.46 flash image (often named flash_v1246.bin)

Place them next to the emulator as roms/boot.bin and roms/flash.bin,
or pass the paths on the command line.

Dumps are often archived on the Internet Archive:

  https://archive.org/details/cybiko

This project does not host CyOS or boot ROMs.
